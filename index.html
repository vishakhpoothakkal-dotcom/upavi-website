ABOUT US

Welcome to UPAVI

UPAVI brings together Malayali students at the Central University of Andhra Pradesh by fostering cultural pride, leadership, academic excellence and lifelong friendships.

Building friendships beyond classrooms.

Community First

Creating a welcoming environment where every Malayali student feels connected, supported and valued from day one.

Cultural Events

Hosting Onam, Kerala Piravi, Vishu, Christmas, Ramzan, Easter, Valentine's Day, cultural festivals, workshops and community events that create lasting memories throughout the academic year.

Academic Support

Helping students with admissions, academics, accommodation and making campus life easier for everyone.

OUR PURPOSE

Mission & Vision

What drives UPAVI today, and the community we aspire to build together for tomorrow.

Mission

To bring Malayali students at CUAP together through culture, friendship, leadership and academic support, creating a connected community where every student can thrive.

Vision

To build a vibrant, inclusive and forward-looking student community that celebrates Kerala's heritage while empowering every member to learn, lead and grow together.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Our Core Values

Community

Creating a welcoming community where every student feels connected, supported and valued.

Culture

Celebrating Kerala's heritage while embracing diversity, friendship and togetherness.

Leadership

Encouraging students to take initiative, lead with purpose and create positive change.

Growth

Supporting academic, personal and professional growth through shared opportunities.

Together, we celebrate where we come from, support where we are, and build where we are going.
